## Fixed

- **AppImages of packages with glob sources are laid out correctly.** An entry such as `src: "dist/*/app"` with `dst: /usr/bin/` installs `/usr/bin/app` in a DEB or RPM, but the AppImage built by 0.8.0 wrote the program to a file called `/usr/bin` and then failed to find it. A glob source, or a destination ending in a slash, now names a directory in the AppImage too. Configurations without globs were not affected. By @crmne.
